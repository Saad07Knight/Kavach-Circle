01 // The problem

Digital arrest is India's costliest fear scam

Fraudsters pose as CBI, police or customs officers on a video call, accuse the victim of a crime and keep them "under arrest" on camera until they transfer their savings.

₹19,000 Cr+reported losses in 2025, India's 3rd-largest fraud category by money lost

02 // Anatomy of the attack

It doesn't hack the phone. It hacks the person

Every step runs on fear and isolation. Steps 4 and 5 leave a clear pattern on the victim's own phone, and that is where Kavach steps in.

03 // The rule engine

Three signals. One rule anyone can read

No machine learning and no training data. A fixed rule runs on the phone, so every alert can be explained as "3 of 3 conditions met".

Long video or voice call

30+ minutes, read from Android's audio mode and camera/mic-in-use state.

Banking or UPI app opened mid-call

Matched against a list of about 50 bank and UPI apps using app-usage stats.

Bank OTP or debit alert arrives

Only the sender and keywords are checked. Messages are never read or stored.

→
// runs on-device, every 5 seconds
if call.active
   and call.minutes >= 30
   and bankAppOpened(duringCall)
   and otpReceived(duringCall):

    alertGuardians(silent=true)
    autoCallVictim(from=guardian)
    flagTransaction(hold="24h")
04 // What makes us different

Six features we found in no consumer scam app

Each one is built for the moment a scammer is directing the victim live, not for the moment after the money is gone.

Duress PIN

A second, fake PIN. If the scammer forces the victim to "turn off" the app, it looks switched off to them and silently alerts the family.

Two-person rule

A large transfer made during a long call needs a guardian to approve it from their own phone.

Isolation breaker

The guardian's phone automatically calls the victim and breaks the scammer's hold.

Invisible to screen share

Kavach screens are blocked from screen sharing and recording, so the scammer never sees it working.

Privacy-first

No audio, message text or amounts are read. All checks run on the phone.

Tamper alarm

If the app is uninstalled, disabled or goes silent, the guardians are told at once.

06 // Security architecture

Built so the protector can't become the target

No static API keys and no home-made encryption. Our own protocol, built on proven primitives.

Victim's phone

Key pair created inside the phone's secure hardware. The private key never leaves it.

→signed + nonce

Kavach relay

Checks the signature and nonce, then forwards a blob it cannot read.

→end-to-end

Guardian's phone

The only device that can decrypt the alert.

No static API keysEvery request is signed by the device itself.

In-person QR pairingA scammer can't add themselves as a guardian remotely.

Hardware-backed keysAndroid Keystore; keys can't be exported.

24-hour guardian-change delayExisting guardians are notified of any change.

Replay-proof requestsOne-time nonce plus timestamp on every call.

Root and tamper detectionObfuscated code, minimal permissions, no accessibility service.

Certificate pinningTLS 1.3, and only to our own server.

Proven primitivesECDSA P-256 signatures, ECDH key exchange, AES-256-GCM.

Get the app

Install Kavach Circle on two phones

One app for both people: the person to protect and their guardian. Android 8 or newer. The phones link over signed SMS, so no internet or account is needed.

STEP 1

Download on both phones

Open the download link in Chrome. When Android asks, allow installing apps from Chrome. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details, then Install anyway.

STEP 2

Choose a role

On one phone pick "Protect me" and grant each permission. On the other pick "I'm a guardian".

STEP 3

Link in person

The protected phone shows a QR code; the guardian scans it. Set a PIN and a duress PIN, then turn protection on.
