01 // The problem

Digital arrest is India's costliest fear scam

Fraudsters pose as CBI, police or customs officers on a video call, accuse the victim of a crime and keep them "under arrest" on camera until they transfer their savings.

₹19,000 Cr+reported losses in 2025, India's 3rd-largest fraud category by money lost

02 // Anatomy of the attack

It doesn't hack the phone. It hacks the person

Every step runs on fear and isolation. Steps 4 and 5 leave a clear pattern on the victim's own phone, and that is where Kavach steps in.

01

Fake courier call

"A parcel in your name contains drugs."

02

Transfer to "CBI"

The call is handed to a fake officer.

03

Video "arrest"

Uniforms, logos and a forged warrant.

04

Isolation

"Don't hang up. Tell no one."

05

"Verify your funds"

Transfers are made, FDs broken.

06

Mule accounts

The money is layered and gone.

03 // The rule engine

Three signals. One rule anyone can read

No machine learning and no training data. A fixed rule runs on the phone, so every alert can be explained as "3 of 3 conditions met".

Long video or voice call

30+ minutes, read from Android's audio mode and camera/mic-in-use state.

Banking or UPI app opened mid-call

Matched against a list of popular bank and UPI apps using app-usage stats.

Bank OTP or debit alert arrives

Only the sender and keywords are checked. Messages are never read or stored.

→
// runs on-device, every 3 seconds
if call.active
   and call.minutes >= 30
   and bankAppOpened(duringCall)
   and otpReceived(duringCall):

    pauseScreen(over=paymentApp)
    smsGuardians("ALERT")
    askFamily(reply="YES / NO")

if call.active and screenShareAppOpened():
    smsGuardians("ALERT")  // at once
04 // What makes us different

Eight protections built for the live call

Each one works while a scammer is directing the victim, not after the money is gone. We found the duress PIN and the two-person rule in no consumer scam app.

Duress PIN

A second, fake PIN. If the scammer forces the victim to "turn off" the app, it looks switched off to them and silently alerts the family.

Two-person rule

A payment made during a long call waits for a guardian to reply YES or NO by SMS from their own phone.

Isolation breaker

Guardians are told to call at once. With the guardian app, their phone calls the victim automatically.

Invisible to screen share

Kavach screens are blocked from screen sharing and recording, so the scammer never sees it working.

Privacy-first

No audio, message text or amounts are read. All checks run on the phone.

Tamper alarm

If someone starts removing the app's protection, the guardians are told at once.

Remote-access shield NEW

Opening AnyDesk, TeamViewer or another screen-control app during any call shows a stop screen and alerts the family straight away.

One-tap SOS NEW

If a call feels wrong, one button texts every guardian and phones the first one.

    06 // Security architecture

    Built so the protector can't become the target

    No server, no account and no API keys. There is nothing online for an attacker to breach.

    Protected phone

    The rule engine runs on the phone. No call audio or message text ever leaves it.

    →alert by SMS

    Mobile network

    Plain SMS: works on any phone, with no internet and no app.

    →YES / NO reply

    Guardian's phone

    Replies count only from a saved guardian's number.

    No API keys, no backendNothing to leak, steal or take offline.

    Replies only from saved guardiansA scammer's YES is ignored.

    Approvals expireOne-time code per request, valid for 30 minutes.

    24-hour guardian-change delayExisting guardians are told by SMS first.

    PIN to switch off or remove a guardianStored only as a salted SHA-256 hash.

    Screens hidden from captureKavach shows as black on screen share and recordings.

    On / off signals onlyNo accessibility service, no call recording, no message storage.

    07 // Roadmap

    From hackathon demo to a national safety layer

    PHASE 1 · NOW

    Working prototype

    Android app with the on-device rule engine, pause screen, SMS alerts and YES / NO family approval.

    PHASE 2 · PILOT

    One bank partner

    PHASE 3 · SCALE

    National rails

    NPCI, telecom operators and state cyber cells; automatic 1930 report on every alert.
